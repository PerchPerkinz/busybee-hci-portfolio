Three personas, derived from seven interviews

Each persona below was built bottom-up from the Phase 2 affinity wall rather than from assumption. The footer of every card names the participants and the affinity themes it came from, and the pull quote is a verbatim line from one of those interviews. Layout follows the Xtensio persona template used in class.

How to read these

Grounded in the data: goals, frustrations, bios, quotes, walk-away behavior and the apps each persona actually reaches for all come directly from interview responses. Every claim traces to a note on the affinity wall.

Inferred, not measured: the personality sliders and the numeric levels on the Motivation and Apps bars are our reading of the interviews, not something participants reported. They are directionally supported by what people said but should not be cited as findings.

Adapted from the template: the class template's Motivation axes (Connection, Recognition, Collaboration and so on) and app list (Canvas, Zoom, OneNote) come from a campus-collaboration study. We kept the template's structure and replaced those axes with ones our research actually speaks to.
